"Search for particles beyond the Standard Model with the CMS detector" - CERN-THESIS-2019-080
(2019), defended 8 July 2019. https://repository.cern/records/wtbe3-tsg09

All statistical analyses and software were written independently. The thesis motivates
Supersymmetry as a beyond-Standard-Model extension (a dark-matter candidate in the neutralino
LSP, the lightness of the Higgs, potential force unification) and covers the SUSY searches - the
single-lepton Run I search and the boosted part of the razor analysis, of which I was principal
investigator for the Boosted regime - plus the Pixel Detector calibration and reconstruction work.
