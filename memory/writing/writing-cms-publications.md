Co-author on 1,000+ peer-reviewed publications through the CMS Collaboration (per high-energy-physics
convention, all collaboration members co-author each paper). Personal scientific contributions are
concentrated in the single-lepton and razor-variable SUSY searches and in Pixel Detector calibration
and reconstruction. Part of the body of work recognised by the 2025 Breakthrough Prize in
Fundamental Physics. Author page: https://inspirehep.net/authors/1069992. Key first-hand
papers have their own writing-paper-* entries.
