A published LinkedIn post arguing that the biggest productivity gain was not a new model but
teaching agents to navigate a curated knowledge layer over ~50 repositories rather than
re-analysing code every session - lower token usage, faster execution, better architectural
consistency, and larger cross-repository tasks. Core lines: "Context is expensive; well-structured
knowledge compounds," and "teaching agents what not to load is often more valuable than access to
everything." This post's engagement led to the NVIDIA EMEA approach.
