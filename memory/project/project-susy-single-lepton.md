A single-lepton Supersymmetry search on the 2012 LHC dataset that produced the world's strongest exclusion limits on the targeted SUSY models at the time of publication. My contribution to this collaborative analysis was measuring the lepton (electron and muon) efficiencies used by the search - a focused part of the effort, not the full analysis. Repositories used for the search: my lepton-efficiency measurements in [RA4Efficiencies](https://github.com/jkarancs/RA4Efficiencies) and [SusyAnalysis](https://github.com/jkarancs/SusyAnalysis).
