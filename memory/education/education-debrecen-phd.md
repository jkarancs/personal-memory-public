PhD in Physics (Sep 2010 - Sep 2019; defended 8 July 2019). Thesis: "Search for particles beyond
the Standard Model with the CMS detector" (CERN-THESIS-2019-080). Research focus: Supersymmetry
searches plus Pixel Detector calibration and offline reconstruction software within the CMS
Experiment at CERN. All statistical analyses and software in the thesis were written
independently. The doctorate overlapped with employment as a Research Assistant at MTA Atomki.
