I am a Senior Software Engineer with a PhD in Physics. I studied Physics at the University of
Leeds (integrated BSc + MSc, entirely in English as a non-native speaker) and earned my PhD at
the University of Debrecen while working within CERN's CMS Collaboration - one of the largest
scientific efforts on Earth (~5,000 physicists, 180+ institutions).

For a decade I contributed to CMS: I developed Pixel Detector offline reconstruction and
calibration software, wrote a full Supersymmetry analysis framework in C++ single-handedly, and
measured the lepton efficiencies for a single-lepton SUSY search. Both analyses set world-best exclusion
limits at the time of publication. I served four consecutive years as a Level 3 Coordinator, ran
distributed analyses across tens of thousands of CPU cores on the Worldwide LHC Computing Grid,
and delivered well over a hundred scientific presentations. I am a co-contributor to the body of
work recognised by the 2025 Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics.

In 2021 I pivoted into commercial software engineering, leveraging my C++/Python background, and
was promoted from Junior to Senior in under four years. I build AWS-native backend microservices
(C# .NET, Python, DynamoDB, SQS, Kafka, Redis, EKS, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, ArgoCD). Most
recently I have become an internal pioneer of AI-augmented engineering: a structured agentic
knowledge base (AGENTS.md methodology) across ~50 repositories, multi-agent orchestration, and a
company-wide AI adoption strategy co-developed with leadership.
