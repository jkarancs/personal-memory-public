**Updated 2026-07-14:** availability has grown. Now roughly **2 hours per day on most days (~6 of 7)**, and around **4 hours on weekends/holidays**. As a result the AI roadmap has been progressing at a much faster pace than originally expected.

Earlier baseline (2026-07-02): 1-2 hours/day on 2-4 days/week, sometimes more at weekends, mostly evenings, fitted around a full-time job and family.

Takes notes and builds learning notebooks with AI. Local hardware is a 6GB GTX 1660 Ti (Turing, no BF16/Tensor Cores) - hence small models, 4-bit QLoRA, batch size 1, FP16, and renting a GPU by the hour for larger runs.
